## Q10: What vendor security requirements apply to third-party contractors?
**Answer:** Each state agency entering into or renewing a contract with a vendor authorized to access, transmit, use, or store data for the agency shall include a provision in the contract requiring the vendor to meet the security controls the agency determines are proportionate with the agency's risk under the contract based on the sensitivity of the agency's data. The vendor must periodically provide to the agency evidence that the vendor meets the security controls required under the contract (Security Control Standards Catalog v2.2.pdf, SA-04: Acquisition Process, p.152).

## Q15: Summarize all policies related to remote work in one paragraph.
**Answer:** The policies related to remote work include the requirement to determine and document organization-defined alternate work sites for employees, implement specific controls at these sites, assess their effectiveness, and ensure communication channels with information security and privacy personnel in case of incidents (Security Control Standards Catalog v2.2.pdf, PE-17: Alternate Work Site, p.109). Additionally, there are guidelines for remote access that mandate the establishment and documentation of usage restrictions, configuration requirements, and authorization for each type of remote access prior to connection (Security Control Standards Catalog v2.2.pdf, AC-17: Remote Access, p.19). Furthermore, there are controls to prohibit remote activation of collaborative computing devices and applications, with specified exceptions, and a requirement to provide users with an explicit indication of use (Security Control Standards Catalog v2.2.pdf, SC-15: Collaborative Computing Devices And Applications, p.165).
